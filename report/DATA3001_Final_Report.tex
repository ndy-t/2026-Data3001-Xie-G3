\item \textbf{Where history matters most.} Among high-speed drifters, the mean error reduction from adding history was 7.03 km for High-turning forecasts, compared with 4.02 km for Low/Medium-turning forecasts. The \emph{between-group difference} was 3.02 km (95\% drifter-cluster bootstrap interval: 1.21--5.25 km). This comparison was selected after inspecting the data and should be treated as exploratory. These are situations in which persistence of the current velocity fails quickly while the drifter still travels far, so a model that ignores recent history is penalised most.
